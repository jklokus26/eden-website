What is Silvia?

Your financial life. One place to understand it.

Silvia is a personal AI CFO that helps users organize their finances and ask questions grounded in their own financial context. It brings assets, liabilities and connected accounts into a consolidated view.

Users can track net worth, explore portfolio concentration and fees, review cash flow and compare scenarios. Silvia is built for independent investors, including entrepreneurs and professionals managing money across multiple accounts and asset types.

Questions, answered

Silvia, explained.

What is Silvia?

Silvia is a personal AI CFO that brings financial accounts, assets and liabilities into one view. It helps people understand their financial position and explore questions using their own financial context.

Who is Silvia for?

Silvia is built for independent investors who want a clearer view of their money. It is particularly relevant to people managing finances across multiple accounts and asset types, including entrepreneurs and professionals with complex financial lives.

What can you do with Silvia?

You can track net worth, examine portfolio concentration and fees, explore financial scenarios, and ask questions about cash flow and investments. Visit Silvia’s personal AI CFO to explore the product.

How did Eden Communications help Silvia?

Eden Communications helped Silvia gain media attention. This case study highlights the partnership and the opportunity to explain AI-powered personal finance to wider audiences.

Does Silvia manage or move your money?

No. Silvia does not directly manage user assets or move money. Connected accounts remain with their existing institutions. Silvia provides financial information and analysis for educational purposes.
