# Eden Communications — Design

*Colors changed 2026-10-22 to the live site's palette (see Colors). Layout, type and motion as below. Rewritten 2026-10-20 for the Oct 2026 revamp. Source: the homepage mockup Eden approved ("lighter and more like the mockup look", Ebony, 2026-10-20), cross-checked against Eden's own client deck (pale sage #F1F5F0, deep green #0B3D2C, mid green #1E6B4E). The older dark system is in git history.*

**One look, every page (since 2026-10-23), the bold kit on every page (since 2026-10-30).** Every page carries `look-2026` on `<body>`, written in the page's HTML (never added by script, so nothing flashes). Rules for this look are scoped under `.look-2026` and override anything inherited from the older global CSS (`global.css`, `nav.css`, `footer.css`, still loaded by `src/main.js` as the base). Shared pieces live once in `src/styles/look-2026.css` and `src/components/look-2026.js` (page opening, case cards, team portraits and bio dialog, questions list, link rows, contact band); pages add only their own sections. Every `<main>` is on the Eden kit in its bold variant (see The bold variant): the homepage through `initKit` and `initBold`, every inner page through `initBoldPage` (src/components/eden-bold.js). When a page changes, check the homepage and one inner page.

## Overview

Eden should read like a serious advisory firm, not an agency and not a tech product: calm, confident, and expensive in the quiet way. Think of a well-made annual report or a broadsheet's weekend section, printed dark. The live site's near-black and white with its brand gradient used sparingly, generous space, one light serif carrying the headlines, a plain sans carrying everything else. Real photography only (Eden's dinners and licensed architectural stock). Restraint is the design: if a detail doesn't help someone trust Eden faster, it goes.

**One theme, dark.** Eden picked it over the light preview on 2026-10-23 (Alkida: "good to push the dark version of the website for now"); the light palette and its build switch were removed (git history has them).

Every value comes from the live site's code (`deliverables/eden/website-clone/src/styles`) or its DESIGN.md; where those two disagree, the live code wins (Joe, 2026-10-22). Don't add or invent a color. When Eden settles a brand palette, it replaces this section.

- **Paper (#0a0a06):** the page, the live `--bg`. Panels (the form, the bio dialog) use it too, with a hairline edge.
- **Paper-deep (white at 3%):** the live card fill, used for alternate bands (testimonials, the dinners band) and image placeholders.
- **Ink (#ffffff):** headlines, labels that open a section, logos, strong text.
- **Ink-body (white at 88%):** running text (the live hero subtitle).
- **Ink-muted (#9b9b9b):** secondary lines and captions (the live `--accent-grey`). Never over a photo: grey on stonework "blends in too well with the background" (Joe, 2026-10-22).
- **Accent, mint (#13ff99):** focus rings, link hover underlines and field focus borders only. Never a fill, never on figures or labels.
- **Buttons:** white fill (#ffffff), near-black text (#0a0a06), hover #f0f0f0, as the live "Get in Touch" and form button. (The live DESIGN.md says mint; the live code is white, and the code wins.)
- **The gradient, as a hairline only (the signature line, 2026-10-28).** The live site's purple-sky-mint gradient (gradient-purple → gradient-sky → gradient-mint) is Eden's brand motif in exactly one form: a 1px line (see The Eden kit). It sits under every section label, marks a hovered row in the What we do index, and stands as a vertical bar beside each client quote. Nowhere else: never a fill, never on text, never thicker than 1px, never a glow. Every other line stays a neutral hairline. (Joe, 2026-10-22, on the gradient as a text fill: "a little corny, a little childish... rather than professional and buttoned up"; as a hairline it reads as a signature, not a decoration. - **Numbers band:** the 3% white band between two hairlines. Figures white, labels grey.
- **Selection:** purple #951bff with white text (live).
- **Rules:** white at 8% (`--rule`) or 12% (`--rule-strong`), the live border tokens; text underlines at 30% (`--link-rule`).
- **Nav once scrolled:** the live frosted bar, rgba(18, 19, 31, 0.85) with a 12px blur.
- **Form fields:** white at 4% fill, white at 12% border, mint on focus; error text #ff6b6b (all live).
- **Over photos:** the scrim uses stops copied from the live hero overlay (rgba(2, 4, 8, 0.92), rgba(3, 5, 15, 0.7), rgba(2, 4, 10, 0.5)); round controls on photos and video sit on the live frosted-nav color, rgba(18, 19, 31, 0.85).

No glows, neon, glass or drop shadows. Logos are always one color (white).

## Typography

Two families, two weights each, loaded from Google Fonts.

- **Newsreader** (serif, optical sizes) for display, section headlines, card titles, large figures and pull quotes. Weight 300 for display and h2, 400 for h3. Always upright. Never italic in headings.
- **Geist** (sans) for body, navigation, buttons, labels and forms, and for the homepage's figures (see Figures). Weights 400 and 500 only.
- **Labels:** Geist 500, 12px (0.75rem), uppercase, 0.14em tracking, ink-muted. The label that opens a section is the plain grey label with the signature line under it carrying the emphasis (the kit's header, every page since 2026-10-30); `.t-label--dot` (white) is no longer used for section labels. Used sparingly: one above a section at most, never as numbered chapter tags beside a heading.
- **Figures:** Geist 400 with tabular numerals, tracked tight, on every page (the homepage since 2026-10-28, the case cards, case study sidebar and dinner numbers since 2026-10-30), so data reads apart from the serif headlines (Eden's reference sites). Numbers are written exactly as Eden supplies them.
- **Measure:** body lines stay under about 65 characters (`max-width: 36rem` on paragraphs). FAQ answers excepted: they run most of the width at Joe's request (see Questions).
- Headline letter-spacing is negative (-0.01 to -0.025em). Body is neutral. Labels are wide.
- **Rendering:** load Newsreader with its optical-size axis (`opsz`) and set `font-optical-sizing: auto`, so large headlines get the display cut and small text the text cut. Weight 300 and the tight tracking are starting values: check them at real sizes on a real screen. Sizes in rem so browser zoom and text resizing work.

## Layout

- **Container:** max 1280px, gutter `clamp(20px, 4vw, 48px)`.
- **Section rhythm:** major sections get `clamp(80px, 10vw, 136px)` of vertical padding. Compact bands (the proof numbers, the logo rows, the footer) get `clamp(48px, 6vw, 72px)`, as in the mockup, so the page doesn't become a run of identical blocks. Sections are separated by space and hairlines, not boxes.
- **Hero (cinematic, like the live site):** the NYSE photo (later Ebony's sizzle reel, the "landing background video") runs edge to edge under a clear nav, behind the live hero overlay's near-black laid left to right (dark where the text sits, lighter over the facade) and fading into the page at the bottom. The headline in two lines (up to 120px), then the deck, paragraph and buttons, all at the bottom left in about 36rem. The overlay uses the live hero's colors with each stop about 6 points lighter (0.86, 0.65, 0.1; Joe, 2026-10-22: first "lighten that the black overlay like another 10, 15%... so you can actually read the word stock exchange", then "slightly darker... maybe a 5-10%"); its darkest stop holds across the text column (to 30% of the width) so the statues sit beside the words, not behind them. The crop (from the 4000px original) puts "STOCK EXCHANGE" in full to the right of the text on laptops and above the headline on phones. The hero fills about the first screen (94svh, 620-1000px); on short laptop screens the headline shrinks so the buttons stay in the first screen. On laptops from 901 to 1279px the text column narrows to 25rem and the overlay's dark stop ends sooner, so "STOCK" clears the paragraph. Phones use a tall crop with the facade and the inscription at the top: the headline starts at least 270px below the nav (on short phones the hero runs past the first screen), and the shade behind the words is drawn by the text block itself (it starts 48px above the headline), so it never covers the inscription at any screen height. The wide photo ships up to 3600px for large retina screens. The nav is clear over the hero and turns into the live frosted bar once the page scrolls. (Joe, 2026-10-22, on the framed editorial hero in dark: "not crazy about this landing section".) Text contrast over the photo is checked at every width (worst-case pixel under each line): every line is above 7.5:1.
- **Grid:** 12 columns on desktop. Statement sections run headline on the left (7 columns) and supporting text on the right (4 columns, offset 1). Three-up rows (industries, the case cards on Our Results) use equal columns; the homepage's Selected work is deliberately not one (see Components).
- **Breakpoints:** 640px, 900px, 1200px. Rows choose their own tablet layout (three-up rows can go to two plus one, logo grids to 3 then 2 columns); everything is one column below 640px.
- **Images:** square corners, fixed aspect ratios so nothing shifts while loading. Case study images are 16:9 and industry images 3:2. Every photo file is at least as wide as its largest retina display size (about 2x the CSS width), so nothing is upscaled into blur: take the largest original available, never a small crop of a small file. Each industry photo has to say its industry at a glance (Joe, 2026-10-21: a building "doesn't make any sense with digital assets"): the NYSE trading floor for Financial Services, the Stellar conference stage for Digital Assets, RoboStrategy's humanoid robot ringing the Nasdaq closing bell for Frontier Technology (see Industries below). Crops are complete compositions (no sculpture sliced at the edge), dark photos are brightened until their subject reads, and nothing shows a dated price or a third-party app brand. Never grey stone that reads as black and white.
- **Anchors:** `scroll-margin-top: 96px` so the fixed nav never covers a heading.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Depth comes from photography, paper tone shifts and hairlines. No shadows, no cards floating on cards.

## Shapes

- Buttons: 2px radius. Rectangular, not pills.
- Images, sections and dividers: square.
- The only round shapes are focus-ring corners (follow the element) and the circular LinkedIn icon hit area.

Eden's Director of Ops asked for a site that feels "genuinely designed for the brand" rather than templated. Her four references (Room & Pillar, Cypher Capital, Cognichip, Inflect) share one move: a handful of pieces repeated everywhere. The kit is those pieces, built once in `src/styles/eden-kit.css` and `src/components/eden-kit.js`. A page opts in by importing the module and calling `initKit` on its `<main>` (the nav and footer sit outside it and keep their own styles). Every page is on it since 2026-10-30, in the bold variant.

- **Signature line (the motif):** the brand gradient as a 1px hairline (see Colors). Its color stops live in one custom property, `--kit-line` (src/styles/eden-kit.css), which every line draws from in its own direction. Eden picked the bold homepage with the gradient (2026-10-29); a plain line, if they ask for one later, is a change to `--kit-line` alone (the calm option showed it in ink-muted grey). It draws left to right the first time its section enters (scaleX from 0, origin left, 700ms, `cubic-bezier(0.3, 0, 0, 1)`).
- **Section header (one system, every section but the hero):** the label (`t-label`, grey), the signature line under it, then the serif headline where the section has one, whose words rise from clipped masks (750ms, 80ms apart, same curve). One column, never label-left and heading-right; an action link may sit at the label's right end. Every section has its label from Eden's existing copy (the team's is its heading, "The team"; the contact band's is its button label, "Talk to Eden"). Built by `sectionHead()`.
- **Interaction language (the ink box):** on mouse hover a solid white box wipes in from the left behind a row or text link and the text over it turns near-black exactly at the box's edge (an inked copy laid over the original and revealed with clip-path; 260ms in, about 180ms out, so it retreats quicker than it arrives). ## Motion

Calm, but the page should feel alive (Alkida, 2026-10-21: "more dynamic and visual"). Everything that moves on its own has a pause control or stops when it leaves the screen, and phones get the same content without depending on hover.

- **Reveal:** content blocks fade in and rise 12px over 500ms with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` when they enter the viewport, staggered 80ms within a group. Once only.
- **Hover:** color and border changes over 150-200ms. The only hover movement is a 3px arrow nudge, on mouse devices only, plus on kit pages the ink box's wipe (clip-path, see The Eden kit). No scale on cards, no tilt, no parallax.
- **Kit motion (kit pages):** the signature line draws once per section (700ms), headline words rise once (750ms, 80ms stagger), quote bars draw top to bottom with their quote, and the lede fills with scroll. All of it is listed under The Eden kit.
- **Figures:** the three proof numbers count up from zero over 1.6s (ease-out, 120ms apart) the first time they scroll into view. Screen readers get the final number only.
- **Reduced motion:** with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (also when switched on mid-visit), nothing moves: reveals show immediately, numbers show their final value, the strips become static grids, the film waits on its poster for a press of play, smooth scrolling is off, and on kit pages the signature lines show drawn, headline words show in place and the lede is plain white.
- Animate `transform` and `opacity` only; kit pages also use `clip-path` for the ink box and the lede (see The Eden kit).

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep copy exactly as Eden supplies it. Numbers too.
- Do use real photographs only, all in color, in one warm, slightly desaturated grade (`--photo-grade`), TV frames included. A heavier mute reads as faded. No black-and-white photos: they read as a different site.
- Do let space do the separating. Hairlines before boxes, boxes almost never.
- Do keep every logo one ink color and optically balanced.
- Don't use italic headings, glows, drop shadows, pills or rounded cards. The brand gradient only where Colors allows it.
- Don't add a third typeface or any color the live site doesn't use.
- Don't use stock clichés (handshakes, laptops, coins, circuit boards) or AI-generated images.
- Don't use a photo in the hero that doesn't say what Eden does. A detail shot (a place setting, a glass) reads as a restaurant. The hero shows the world Eden works in (New York's financial district) until Eden's own video replaces it.
- Don't use numbered chapter labels beside headlines, or icon rows in circles.
- Don't animate layout properties, and don't animate anything that the reader didn't scroll to (one exception on kit pages: the hero headline's single rise as the page opens).
- Don't change these values as a side effect of another task. Change them on purpose, then update this file.
